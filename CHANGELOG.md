# Changelog

## Unreleased

## [0.17.0] - 2026-11-25

Reg M-D arrives with its new Pokémon, Megas and the December 2 balance changes. Teams on earlier regulations keep their old values.

### New
- **Teams:** Reg M-D is now a selectable regulation, with its own badge color and filters on Teams and profiles.
- **Teams:** Reg M-D adds Heatran, Dondozo and all three Tatsugiri forms. A plain "Tatsugiri" in an import is read as Curly.
- **Teams:** Mega Heatran and Mega Tatsugiri are in. Heatranite and Tatsugirinite are holdable, and the Mega toggle shows Water Compaction and Premium Grade.
- **Teams:** Reg M-D teams can pick the December 2 movepool additions: Psych Up for 17 Pokémon including every Rotom form, Slack Off for Emboar, Roost for Beedrill and Hydreigon, Tailwind for Emolga, Wonder Room for Umbreon, and Play Rough for all seven Eeveelutions.
- **Import:** Pokepaste imports that name Reg M-D in their notes now set the regulation automatically.
- **Battle Stats:** Ranked seasons M-7 and M-8 are now recognized.

### Changed
- **Moves:** On Reg M-D, Screech, Metal Sound, Steel Wing and Frost Breath have 100% accuracy. Rock Slide is 95% with a 20% flinch chance.
- **Abilities:** On Reg M-D, Flash Fire's boost now lasts through Mega Evolution, and Stalwart's moves hit through substitutes.
